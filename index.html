How it works

  1. Select a candidate region or enter coordinates.
  2. Choose a planning date and hour.
  3. The demo model estimates simplified Sun and Earth angles.
  4. Review scenarios and export a mission summary.
  5. For research-grade results, integrate SPICE/Horizons geometry and LOLA terrain horizons.
